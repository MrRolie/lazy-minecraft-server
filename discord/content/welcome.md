# Welcome 👋

This is our Minecraft server. The idea is **Vanilla Extended**: Minecraft as if Mojang had kept shipping big exploration updates for a few more years. You get better terrain, better dungeons and structures, new bosses, and more reasons to keep playing after the Dragon.

It still plays like the Minecraft you know. There are no quest books, tech trees or wiki homework. If you find something weird, go poke it.

Some of it will be held back and released later as new **chapters**, so the world keeps changing.
---
**House rules**
1. Don't grief or steal. Ask before building right next to someone.
2. If someone's clearly in the middle of exploring a structure, let them finish it.
3. Be decent in chat and voice.
